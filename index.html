CogniCore

A personal tool for making animated explainer videos for one YouTube channel.

CogniCore is a desktop app with a companion phone app. Its owner uses it to write scripts, build animated scenes, record voice-overs, render videos, review drafts on the phone, and upload the finished videos to their own YouTube channel.

It is not a public service: there are no accounts to sign up for and nothing to buy. It connects only to the owner's own Google account (YouTube and one Google Drive folder), and only when the owner signs in.
