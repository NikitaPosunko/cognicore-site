Privacy policy

CogniCore · last updated 10 November 2026

CogniCore is a personal tool used by its owner to make videos for their own YouTube channel. It has no other users. This page says what it does with Google data when the owner signs in.

What Google data it uses, and why

  • YouTube: to upload the owner's videos (always as private first), set their titles, descriptions, captions and thumbnails, read the channel's own numbers (views, watch time, how long viewers stay) and read comments on the owner's own videos.
  • Google Drive: to keep one shared folder in step between the owner's PC and phone (drawings, reviews, reminders, app updates).
  • Basic profile (name, email): to show which account is signed in.

Where it is kept

On the owner's own PC and phone, and in the owner's own Google Drive folder. Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the device. CogniCore has no server of its own and keeps no copy anywhere else.

Who it is shared with

No one. Google user data is not sold, not shared with advertisers, and not used for anything but the features above. When the owner asks an AI assistant inside the app a question (for example, to summarise comments on their own videos), only the text needed for that answer is sent to the AI service the owner has chosen, to answer that request.

CogniCore's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's files on the PC and phone removes everything it stored there.
